Privacy Policy

Digital Card Holder · Effective 10 November 2026

Digital Card Holder (“the app”) is published by DBSL. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app on Android. If you have a question about it, write to claude.dbsl@gmail.com.

In short

  • The cards you save, their details and their photos are stored only on your phone. The app has no account and no server, and we, the publisher, never receive your cards.
  • The text on a card is read on the phone. Photos and card details are not uploaded to read them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google receives some information about your device to show and measure those ads, as described below.

What the app stores on your phone

When you scan, import or type in a card, the app stores what the card holds: names, job titles, companies, phone numbers, email addresses, websites, postal addresses, social media handles, notes and tags you add, and photos of the front and back of the card. It also stores your own card if you create one, and your settings (such as theme and language).

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, and it is not copied to cloud backup by Android. It stays until you delete the card or uninstall the app; uninstalling deletes everything.

Camera and photos

The app asks for permission to use the camera. It uses the camera only while you scan a business card or a QR code. When you choose a photo from your gallery, the app sees only the picture you pick.

Text on a card is recognised on the phone by Google's ML Kit. The photo and the recognised text are not sent to us or to Google. Like other Google libraries, ML Kit may send Google technical information about how it is running, such as the device model, the app version and how long recognition took, to keep the service working and improve it. See Google's ML Kit terms.

Sharing, contacts and exports

A card leaves the app only when you choose to: when you share it (as a picture, PDF, contact file, QR code or text), save it to your phone's contacts, or export your cards as a spreadsheet or contact file. It then goes to the app or person you choose, under that app's own privacy terms. The app cannot read your contact list.

Backups

You can make a backup of all your cards and photos as a ZIP file and save it to your phone, share it (for example to Google Drive) or send it by email. You decide where the file goes. You can protect a backup with a password: it is then encrypted (AES-256) and opens only with that password, which the app does not store and nobody can recover. A backup without a password is not encrypted, so anyone who gets the file can read the cards in it.

Advertising

The list of cards and each card's page show a banner ad, the full list of cards shows an ad among every ten cards, and from time to time a full-screen ad appears after a card is saved. The ads are served by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device and the app, such as model, operating system and language,
  • how you interact with the ads, and diagnostic information.

Your cards, photos and contacts are never shared with Google or any advertiser.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your choice before any ad is loaded, using Google's consent tool. You can change that choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Read how Google uses this information in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them.

Security

Your cards are protected by Android's app storage: other apps cannot read them. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app and see your cards.

Your choices

  • Delete any card in the app, or uninstall the app to delete everything.
  • Turn the camera permission off in your phone's settings; you can still add cards by hand or from contact files.
  • Change your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices (where offered), and reset your advertising ID in Android's settings.

Because we do not receive or keep your cards, there is nothing about you on our side to request, correct or delete.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
